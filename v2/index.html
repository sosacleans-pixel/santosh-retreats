The Santosh journey

Five days, four movements.

01020304
  1. 01

    Arrive

    Round-trip airport transfers on the scheduled days bring you straight to boutique lodging steps from the beach. Share your flight details and the team coordinates the rest, so you simply show up.

  2. 02

    Move

    Sunrise yoga, functional strength sessions and breathwork, at your own pace. Then adventure: horseback riding, ziplining, canyoning, surfing and boat days. No pressure, no performance.

  3. 03

    Restore

    Balance the adventure with deep rest: ice baths, sauna, massage therapy and sound healing, plus chef-prepared, locally inspired meals shared with a view.

  4. 04

    Transform

    Evenings are for reflection and honest conversation. You leave feeling balanced and fully alive, and you take that energy and clarity back into everyday life.

Meet the founder

Movement with meaning.

Salim Heyaime began his career as a civil engineer in the Dominican Republic, working on public infrastructure and development projects, and later ran businesses there. He was never the athletic type. In his mid-30s, personal challenges showed him how deeply movement, nutrition and mindset are connected, and that discovery changed the direction of his life.1

Roughly nine years of training himself and coaching others later, he helps people in Utah and around the world through online coaching, and he wrote A Challenge to Move: Your 22-Day Roadmap to Move Better and Feel Better, a 7-minute-a-day habit system.1

Santosh Retreats grew out of the same belief: healing happens when we slow down, reconnect and remember who we are. Whether he is leading sunrise yoga, guiding a group through a river canyon or talking by the fire, Salim walks beside guests as a fellow traveler.

"Nothing I do is cookie-cutter."Salim Heyaime, Voyage Utah1
