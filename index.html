Who it's for

Come as you are.

Guests typically range from their mid-20s to early 50s, and every adult is welcome. What matters is the mindset: openness to connection, rest, growth and a little adventure.

  • Solo travelers

    Most guests join solo, and you're welcomed into a warm, inclusive group from day one.

  • Couples & friends

    Come with a partner, friend or family member, in a shared or private room.

  • Every fitness level

    Connection and presence, not performance. Everything is optional, with no pressure to keep up.

  • Bodies with a story

    Injuries can usually be accommodated. Share them in advance and sessions are adapted for you.

  • Groups & teams

    Families, friends and corporate teams can inquire about a fully custom retreat.

  • Instructors & leaders

    Bring your community and we handle the planning and logistics.

What's included

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

The signature retreat is five days and four nights, and covers the essentials.

Ask what's included
  • 01

    Beachside accommodation

    Boutique lodging steps from the beach, with shared and private rooms.

  • 02

    Daily wellness sessions

    Yoga, functional movement and breathwork.

  • 03

    All meals

    Chef-prepared and locally inspired, with non-alcoholic drinks. Plant-based by default; most dietary needs accommodated.

  • 04

    Adventure excursions

    Horseback riding, ziplining, canyoning, surfing and a boat tour.

  • 05

    Recovery & unique sessions

    Sauna, ice baths, massage, sound healing, dance and a cooking class.

  • 06

    Transfers & transport

    Round-trip airport transfers on the scheduled days, plus transportation to all activities.

  • ✕

    Not included

    Flights. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
